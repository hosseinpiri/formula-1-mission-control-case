## The case

At the end of lap 23 of a 50-lap race, a race strategist in the team's Mission Control room must choose between two policies. The planned policy pits on lap 25, the lap that the team's optimization model says is best. The reactive policy stays out and gambles on a cheap pit stop under a safety car that may or may not appear in the next few laps. The student simulator runs both policies against the same random futures and reports the distribution of outcomes.

The case teaches Monte Carlo simulation, the difference between optimizing for one assumed future and evaluating a policy over many possible futures, and the discipline of judging a decision by its expected value rather than by the single outcome that happened. No coding is required; the simulator is pure Excel.
